Our Story

✦

A Love Letter to
Indian Cuisine

Born from a single conviction: that Indian food deserves a place at the world's finest tables, with the ceremony and reverence it has always merited.

Chef Aryan Malhotra spent twelve years in the kitchens of Paris, Tokyo, and New York before returning home with a burning question — what if we applied the precision of French technique and the restraint of Japanese plating to the breathtaking complexity of Indian spice?

The answer became Saffron & Smoke. Every dish is a dialogue between centuries-old Mughal recipes, regional Indian traditions, and the sharp intuition of a kitchen that has never stopped asking "what if?"
